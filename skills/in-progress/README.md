# In Progress

Skills still in development — not ready to use. Expect rough edges, breaking changes, and abandoned experiments. Excluded from the top-level README and the plugin until they graduate to a stable bucket.

- **[forge](./forge/SKILL.md)** — Forge a high-performance, reusable skill out of an expert's own method. A thin orchestrator over a three-movement spine (Desmontar → Remontar → Transcender) that invokes `/grilling`, `/domain-modeling`, `/skill-creator`, and `/handoff`, keeping only what is genuinely the forge's (the Desmonte, the shared-state chart, the orchestration).
- **[leonardo-method](./leonardo-method/SKILL.md)** — The four-phase deep-study method (desconstrução → captura → experimentação → reconstrução) for truly internalizing structured material instead of just consuming it. The forge's Desmonte is its inverted-polarity sibling.
- **[grilling](./grilling/SKILL.md)** — Relentless one-question-at-a-time interrogation to stress-test a plan or drag a tacit method into explicit form.
- **[antithesis-red-teaming](./antithesis-red-teaming/SKILL.md)** — Assemble a blind panel of lens-specialized adversaries to stress-test a plan, design, or decision and surface what breaks before reality does, then synthesize a ranked, traceable verdict.
